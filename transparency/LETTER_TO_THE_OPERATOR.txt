Dear Operator,

If you are listening to or reading this message, know that this operating 
system was designed and built specifically for you.

AnnonOS is a sovereign, hardware-rooted, zero-trust system—crafted to guarantee 
your privacy, security, and independence in the field. But more importantly, 
it was built with deep accessibility at its core, not as an afterthought.

You should know who built this system and how it came to be:

This operating system was engineered and brought to life with the assistance 
of Artificial Intelligence by a mechanical engineer and forensic consultant who 
is himself blind. 

Because of that shared experience, every feature in AnnonOS—from the voice-biometric 
gateway and the audible step-and-bus transit calculator, to the hands-free command 
directory and silent haptic cues—was designed with an exact understanding of what 
it means to navigate the world without sight.

You are holding a system that respects your sovereignty, protects your medical 
and personal privacy behind military-grade encryption, and ensures that you 
always have complete, independent control over your technology.

You are never alone, and your tools were built to serve you faithfully.

To hear your available voice commands at any time, simply say:
"anon list commands"

To hear a report on your system's security and health, say:
"anon system report"

With respect and solidarity,

The Chief Engineer & The AnnonOS Development Team
